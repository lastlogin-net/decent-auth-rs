# Repository guidance

`project.md` is the project's durable, high-level memory. Keep it concise and update it when substantial work changes the current status, design direction, or an important decision or trade-off.

It should summarize completed milestones, current project status, significant decisions, and future plans. Do not record unresolved exploitable security findings, reproduction details, or other sensitive security backlog items there; keep those in the private security review until they are mitigated. Once an issue is addressed, its outcome and any lasting design decision may be summarized in `project.md`.
